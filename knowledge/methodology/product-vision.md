# Knowledge-Driven Engineering Product Vision

## Goal

Reduce engineering mistakes by making important system knowledge explicit, versioned, traceable, and cheap to retrieve.

## Users

- Staff Engineers
- Senior Engineers
- Technical leads
- AI implementation agents

## Product Thesis

Code represents part of system knowledge. Teams also need durable records for product intent, design constraints, architectural decisions, behavior specs, and operational learning.

## Usefulness Test

An artifact earns its place when it helps a human or agent avoid a wrong change, avoid rediscovering a decision, or find the minimum canonical context for work.
