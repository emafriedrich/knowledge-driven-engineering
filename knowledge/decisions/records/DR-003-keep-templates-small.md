# DR-003: Keep templates small

## Context

Large templates make contributors fill sections because the template asks for them. This increases maintenance cost and encourages stale text.

## Decision

Keep templates short and sectioned around the artifact's primary question.

## Consequences

Authors must decide whether a document deserves more detail. The methodology avoids default bureaucracy, but reviewers may need to ask for missing context when risk is high.
