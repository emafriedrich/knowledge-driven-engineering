# Food Delivery Storefront Product Context

## Product Goal

Help local customers find a meal they want and place an order with low friction.

## Audience

Primary users are customers ordering food from nearby restaurants. Many arrive without a specific restaurant in mind.

## Product Principle

The storefront should support intent-first discovery. If a customer knows they want pizza, empanadas, or sushi, the product should let them start from that category before scanning restaurants.
