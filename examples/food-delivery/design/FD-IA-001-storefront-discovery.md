# Storefront Discovery Information Architecture

## Concepts

- `Category`: a food type such as pizza, empanadas, sushi, burgers, or desserts.
- `Restaurant`: a merchant that can accept orders.
- `Restaurant list`: the set of restaurants available for the selected delivery context.

## Storefront Structure

1. Location or delivery context.
2. Food categories.
3. Restaurant list.
4. Restaurant card details.

## Notes

Information architecture does not define card styling, spacing, or animation. Design-system artifacts own reusable visual rules.
